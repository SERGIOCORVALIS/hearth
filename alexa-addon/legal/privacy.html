Hearth Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Hearth is a hackathon demonstration of an Alexa+ MCP family evening concierge. The local demo stores household planning data in memory on the machine running the MCP server. It does not sell personal data.

When you connect Hearth to Alexa+ through a tunnel, requests may include utterances and tool arguments needed to plan an evening. Do not submit real medical data or sensitive identifiers in the demo.

Contact for this demo project: the repository maintainers listed on the project's GitHub page.
